10. Set-piece events
10.1 The Policy Vote — “The Solidarity Tax” (TM 124)
•           The proposal: a 15% tax on the free cash of the top 10% of players by free cash, distributed equally among the bottom 20% by free cash.
•           The base is free cash, not total wealth. This is deliberate and must be stated when the vote is called.
•           Current free-cash ranks are published when the proposal is announced, so the rich are publicly outed.
•           Voting is democratic and open for 90 seconds. Simple majority passes.
•           The escape route is legal: a rich player can buy inventory to cut free cash before the snapshot. Say nothing about it. The snapshot is taken the moment the vote closes, not when it was called.
•           Expect retaliation. If the tax passes, wealthy players with large inventory often weaponise it against the markets the beneficiaries are trading in. Let them.
10.2 The Government Grant (announced TM 147, awarded TM 157)
•           $10,000, split equally among all players tied at the highest Aerium inventory at the moment of the award.
•           The split is the v2 fix: with a 100-unit cap, several players reaching exactly 100 is the normal outcome, not an edge case.
•           Intended effect: it deliberately breaks fair-value trading. Players bid Aerium up to win a share, creating a predictable bubble; elite players short the top, knowing demand vanishes the second the grant is awarded.
•           Announce the winners and the per-player amount publicly.
10.3 The Final Squeeze (TM 172)
Triple every bot’s volatility parameter. Spreads widen massively, depth thins, and any player who still needs to exit a large position pays for it. This is the last 10 minutes: expect margin calls.
